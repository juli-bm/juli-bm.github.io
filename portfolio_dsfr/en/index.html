About

Master's student in Editorial Web and UX Strategy, I'm interested in designing digital experiences that are accessible, useful, and tailored to users' needs.

My interest in digital design comes with a broader curiosity for creative fields. Design, art, architecture, and video games are all sources of inspiration that feed my UX and UI design practice.

Through my projects, I aim to balance needs analysis, accessibility, and user experience quality in order to design interfaces that are both functional and enjoyable to use.
